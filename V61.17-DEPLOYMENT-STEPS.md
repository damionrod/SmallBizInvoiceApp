# V61.17 Deployment Steps

1. Deploy the contents of this package to Netlify.
2. No SQL migration is required.
3. Open Payroll and confirm the permanent tabs are: Employees, Timesheets, Pay Runs, New Pay Run, Settings.
4. Open Payroll > Settings and confirm:
   - Pay Run Defaults are editable for the current business.
   - Tax & Statutory Rates are visible and read-only.
   - KiwiSaver & ACC rates are visible and read-only.
   - Pay Items can be added/edited/archived.
   - Leave Types and Document Types remain available.
5. Confirm Super Admin > Country Payroll Tax Rules still manages the centrally controlled statutory rates.

Scope: Payroll Settings UI only. No payroll calculations, tax logic, pay runs, timesheets, payslips, emails, CSVs or other modules were changed.
