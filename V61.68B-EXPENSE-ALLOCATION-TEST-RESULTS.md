# V61.68B Expense Business-Use Allocation — Test Results

## Exact baseline
The implementation used the exact ZIP attached in the implementation turn: `finlo-v61.68B-central-settings-cache-fix(2).zip`. The supplied specification names `(1).zip`; no `(1).zip` was attached in that turn, so no earlier remembered build was substituted.

## Scope implemented
Mobile-first whole-bill business/private allocation with Percentage and Business amount modes. Original supplier bill/payment fields retain their existing meanings. Financials/GST and the narrowly approved Job Profitability compatibility path use the business portion. Bank Reconciliation and supplier payments remain on original bill values. AI scanning continues to extract the original supplier bill and does not infer allocation.

## Calculation tests
PASS.
- $200 / 100%: business $200.00, private $0.00, business GST $26.09.
- $200 / 30%: business $60.00, private $140.00, business ex GST $52.17, business GST $7.83.
- $200 / $75 business amount: 37.5%, business $75.00, private $125.00.
- $200 / no GST / 30%: business $60.00, business GST $0.00, private $140.00.
- Percentage is constrained to 0–100 in UI and database.
- Business/private amounts have non-negative DB constraints; client save rejects business amount outside 0–original bill.

## Financials / GST Return
PASS (static logic + calculation simulation). Invoice-basis Financials uses allocated business ex-GST/GST/total. Split lines are proportionally allocated at the whole-bill business-use ratio. Payments-basis GST retains payment timing against the original bill while applying the business-use ratio to deductible GST. 100% historical expenses fall back to existing values.
