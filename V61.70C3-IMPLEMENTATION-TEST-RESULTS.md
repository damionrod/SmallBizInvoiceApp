# V61.70C3 — Customer Refund & Bank Reconciliation

## Implementation
- Protected customer_refunds with RF numbering, Draft → Recorded → Voided lifecycle and tenant-scoped SELECT.
- Customer-level available credit = gross valid credit − Recorded refunds.
- Recording uses a per-business/customer advisory transaction lock and rechecks available credit.
- Accounting-backed refund: Dr Accounts Receivable / Cr Payment Clearing.
- Bank match: links the existing Recorded refund; Dr Payment Clearing / Cr mapped Bank only when the refund is accounting-backed.
- Refund reconciliation Undo leaves the Recorded refund intact and reverses only the latest posted bank-evidence journal.
- Re-match uses the next bank posting_version, preventing an earlier reversed bank journal from suppressing a later legitimate match.
- Recorded unreconciled refund can be controlled-voided with a reason; reconciled refund requires Undo first.
- Frontend roles have SELECT-only access to customer_refunds; financial mutations are RPC-only.
- TRUNCATE removed from anon/authenticated on targeted Bank Reconciliation tables.
- Accountant Pack exports Customer Credit Notes and Customer Refunds separately from bank evidence.
