# V61.68B Settings IA — Simulation & Regression Results

Centralise the existing tenant settings experience into the existing full-page `view-settings` SPA view. Existing Invoice and Job Costing settings controls and handlers are reused; their module Settings tabs are shortcuts to the same central controls. Add tenant Tax & GST editing using the existing `financial_settings` and business settings fields.
