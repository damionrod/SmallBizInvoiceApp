V61.78 — Simplified Financials and Reports Navigation

Scope
- Information architecture, placement, labels and responsive navigation only.
- No database migration.
- No accounting/report calculation rewrite.

Financials
- Five primary choices: Summary, Profit & Loss, Money In & Out, GST, Budget.
- More financial reports: Balance Sheet, Cash Book, General Ledger, Trial Balance.
- Cash Flow visible label changed to Money In & Out; existing cash-flow calculation is unchanged.
- Financials header now includes For My Accountant.

Reports
- Five primary choices: Business Performance, Sales & Money Owed, Expenses & Bills, Jobs, Payroll.
- Monthly Revenue, Expenses & Margin moved once from Sales to Business Performance; existing renderer/data path unchanged.
- Sales keeps sales metrics, month-on-month sales, Invoices Issued & Paid, monthly sales table and Aged Receivables.
- Expenses keeps expense metrics, Monthly Expenses, Expenses by Category, Expenses by Supplier and Aged Payables.
- Expenses by Job moved once to Jobs.
- Jobs routes to the existing Job Costing profitability implementation for estimated/actual costs, revenue, profit and margin; no duplicate profitability logic was created.
- Recent Expenses remains in Reports because expenses.js currently renders that DOM target directly; removing it without changing the existing Expenses implementation would risk a regression. The underlying My Expenses list remains unchanged.

For My Accountant
- Removed Accountant Centre from the ordinary Reports tab row.
- Renamed visible entry to For My Accountant.
- Accessible from Financials header and account popover; both open the same existing Accountant Centre panel.
- Year-end readiness moved from GST to For My Accountant and relabelled Check My Records; existing readiness function and IDs retained.

Permissions and isolation
- Existing Expense and Payroll report entitlement hiding retained.
- Jobs choice follows existing Job Costing navigation entitlement.
- Expenses by Job is hidden when Expenses is unavailable.
- Check My Records is hidden when Financials is unavailable.
- Report and Financials navigation reset to their safe primary view when switching businesses; data access/query logic is unchanged.

Responsive/accessibility
- Five primary navigation choices use a two-column touch-friendly layout on narrow screens rather than compressed horizontal tabs.
- More financial reports uses a native details/summary control with >=44px target, keyboard/touch support and explicit aria-expanded synchronization.
- Menu becomes in-flow on mobile so it cannot open off-screen.
- Existing table horizontal scrolling, chart sizing and pointer-based movable report widgets remain intact.
- Business Performance and Jobs have separate saved widget layout keys.

Files changed (runtime)
- public/index.html
- public/app.js
- public/financials.js
- public/saas.js (cache loader + account-menu route only)
- public/styles.css

Visual description
Desktop Financials: five clear primary tabs with a separate More financial reports control; For My Accountant at the header right.
Mobile Financials: primary choices become large two-column touch targets; More financial reports expands full-width in-flow.
Desktop Reports: five clear operational categories; Business Performance is the default; sales-period controls are shared only by Business Performance and Sales & Money Owed.
Mobile Reports: two-column wrapped navigation with readable labels and no hover dependency; existing tables retain horizontal scrolling.
For My Accountant: dedicated existing Accountant Centre content with Check My Records at the top, followed by accounting period, exports, mapping, validation and history.
