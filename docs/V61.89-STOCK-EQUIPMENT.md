# v61.89 Stock & Equipment — controlled operational foundation

## Scope and design

Existing supplier bills store GST and split lines in `expenses` and `expense_lines`. Source documents live in `expense_attachments`. Invoices store item lines in JSON, without a stock-item foreign key. Jobs and bank matching have their own existing cost/payment paths. Financials and GST consume existing records, so reclassifying or adding a cost-of-sales entry to a recorded purchase would currently double count it. This release does not alter those paths or historical reports.

The new module defaults **off** globally. Super Admin enables it in Modules and grants it through a plan or business override. An entitled business sees Stock & Equipment in navigation. On a phone, use Overview → Review purchases → Stock → Equipment → Activity. Scan and record a bill in Expenses first, then confirm each line in Review purchases; the source document remains accessible in Expenses. Nothing is inferred automatically from a scan. The UI is responsive and uses compact rows; the source document is referenced rather than embedded in the review form.

| Owner choice | Operational action | Accounting / tax action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stock to sell | Increase quantity and estimated value using weighted average cost | Accountant reviews removal from expense, closing stock and cost of sales; not posted automatically |
| Supplies for work | Optional quantity tracking; link usage to a job ID | Accountant reviews duplication with existing job expense |
| Equipment we keep | Save register, original cost and GST source | Book/tax basis, grouping, depreciation, Investment Boost and low-value eligibility need review |
| Regular business expense | Note confirmation | Existing expense and GST remain as recorded |
| Needs review | Hold confirmation | No new accounting treatment |

Stock movements with an invoice ID use the business's existing invoice for reference; sales lines cannot be linked automatically to stock items because the invoice JSON has no stable stock ID. Returns and downward stocktakes update quantities and weighted average value, with actor and reason retained. A disposal stores date and proceeds with an audit event and flags accountant review. The accounting history, tax depreciation, GST and filed periods are never rewritten. The module does **not** automatically post cost of sales, depreciation, balance sheet balances, tax deductions or job actuals, nor export depreciation schedules that have not been computed. Operational CSV is available in Activity.

Opening stock is entered through Add tracked item → Move → Opening quantity and unit cost. Equipment opening requires original cost, GST, opening book and tax values, condition, use percentage, purchase and available dates. No migration of historical expenses occurs. If disabled later, records remain in database and historical accounting results remain untouched; an accountant must explicitly review remaining balances and outstanding schedules before making postings.

## Tax references and review

Official IRD references used for *draft review metadata only*: [Claiming depreciation](https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/income-tax-for-businesses-and-organisations/types-of-business-expenses/depreciation/claiming-depreciation), [Investment Boost](https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/income-tax-for-businesses-and-organisations/types-of-business-expenses/investment-boost), [Trading stock](https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/income-tax-for-businesses-and-organisations/types-of-business-expenses/trading-stock). Draft values of $1,000 and 20% require date, GST, grouping and eligibility review. No IRD rule, rates or part-year calculation is implemented as a tax deduction. The Tax Rules panel records dated drafts, approvals, versions and audit events; “Check IRD updates” opens official guidance. Restore makes another draft for approval. A past-effective rule triggers a manual review of affected assets; the application does not rewrite filed periods. Changes to legal calculation logic require a reviewed software release. This module is not IRD approved.

## Installation, deployment and verification

1. Review the new migration `supabase/migrations/20260924110000_v6189_stock_equipment_foundation.sql` against a **staging clone** of the current schema. Check existing expense line and entitlement columns. No migration or live database operation was run in this workspace.
2. Apply the SQL once in staging; verify RLS with two businesses and different roles, including cancel/expiry. Test bill split, mixed invoice, incorrect classification before confirmation, equipment opening, sale, return, stocktake and disposal there. Only after approval apply to production.
3. Deploy the complete ZIP. Globally enable the `stock_equipment` module in Super Admin → Modules; grant it in Subscription Plans or the business module override. No other module settings change automatically. Audit approval before publishing any tax-rule reference.
4. If staging checks fail, leave this module globally off. Existing flows continue to run. Do not mark financial effects as posted until a separate accountant-reviewed reclassification and posting integration is delivered.

Changed application files: `public/index.html`, `public/app.js`, `public/saas.js`, `public/stock-equipment.js`, `public/stock-equipment.css`. New migration and this document are the remaining additions. No billing, invoice, referral, payroll, bank or existing expense implementations were modified.

## Remaining work before automated accounting

Need reliable invoice-line-to-stock IDs, duplicate-proof sales/returns and expense adjustments; reviewed reclassification from recorded expense to inventory or asset (including GST and private use); locked accounting periods, accounting/tax depreciation schedules and corrections; loss/disposal balancing adjustments; accountant export of actual approved book/tax schedules; IRD rate tables and verification of legal changes; an approved job-cost link without duplicate cost; a review queue for disabled modules with outstanding balances. Until then stock value is an **operational estimate**, not a posted financial balance.
