# V61.88 — Frindly paid plan changes

An owner reviews a Stripe-priced plan change before confirming. An existing
subscription is always updated; a second Checkout subscription is never
created. The bookkeeper retains cancellation permissions but cannot change
plans or undo an owner's scheduled plan change.

- Same-interval upgrade: Stripe previews the current invoice, including any
  proration, discount and tax. Frindly pins the proration time for five minutes,
  checks the quote against Stripe again, immediately invoices with
  `payment_behavior=error_if_incomplete`, and grants the new plan only after
  Stripe confirms the invoice paid. A failed or authentication-required card
  charge leaves the existing plan in place; the owner can update the payment
  method and retry. The renewal date stays the same.
- Same-interval downgrade: the owner sees the next plan price and renewal date.
  A Stripe subscription schedule holds the current plan through its current
  paid period, changes the price at renewal, then releases the schedule while
  the same subscription continues to renew. The owner can undo this scheduled
  change before renewal. Existing records are retained; modules and limits
  change only when Stripe updates the subscription price.
- Monthly to annual is an immediate, previewed, charge-dependent change; the
  billing date may reset. Annual to monthly is scheduled at the end of the
  already-paid annual period. The price/interval and effective date are shown
  before confirmation.
- A current duplicate active Stripe subscription, cancellation in progress,
  failed payment, trialing Stripe subscription, pending invoice update or
  externally-managed schedule blocks unsafe plan mutations with a clear error.
  A customer with custom tax rates or discounts needs a supported manual
  review before a downgrade is scheduled to prevent losing those settings.
- Stripe's portal will not cancel a subscription while its downgrade schedule
  is active. If the owner or bookkeeper confirms cancellation from Frindly in
  this situation, the same schedule is changed to cancel at the current paid
  period end. Either role can subsequently keep the subscription. Ordinary
  cancellations without a scheduled plan change still use the restricted
  Stripe-hosted confirmation flow.

The portal remains responsible for payment methods, history and period-end
cancellation. Connected Stripe invoice payments and the existing invoice
settlement paths are untouched. This release does not cancel, refund, or
modify the two previously identified active Care New Zealand test subscriptions.

Deploy the complete archive to GitHub/Netlify. The `create-checkout` and
`create-portal` Supabase function versions are deployed to the connected test
project. No database migration is required for V61.88. Automated fixtures
cover successful/declined upgrades, stale quotes, scheduled/undone downgrades,
annual changes, duplicate protection, cancellation and invoice-payment
regressions. A live Stripe **test-mode invoice preview** verified the preview
request without creating a charge or changing a subscription; a live test
charge or schedule was deliberately not made against an existing customer.
