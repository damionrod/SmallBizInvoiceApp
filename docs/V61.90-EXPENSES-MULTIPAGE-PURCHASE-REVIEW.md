# v61.90 — bill pages and purchase item review

## What changed

- Expenses accepts several ordered photos or files for one bill, or a PDF containing several pages. The current supplier bill form still saves promptly with its original category, total, GST, payment and attachment records. The existing image reader scans the selected pages together and proposes separate priced items. A user can add more pages to a saved bill, rescan all pages and inspect each attachment.
- A recorded bill has **Review items** in Expenses, even when Stock & Equipment is disabled. A compact desktop row (stacked controls on phones) shows item, category, editable suggested use, quantity, ex GST, GST, and including GST. The category starts from the saved bill; a split bill uses a uniquely matching source line description. Equipment asks for available date, useful life and book method. The whole invoice saves only if ex GST and GST both match the recorded bill exactly.
- The confirmed purchase review is versioned; correcting one needs a reason. A stale save is rejected. A posted bill or closed accounting/GST period rejects a new review. When Stock & Equipment is enabled the existing operational register is updated in the same database transaction. When disabled, only the Expenses review is saved; it creates no optional register entries.
- The Financials **Summary estimate** uses confirmed ordinary expense lines in place of the bill's original category after Save. Before Save, original bill behavior remains. Equipment, small tools, work materials and resale stock are held out of that estimate pending proper movements and accountant treatment. Source GST, payments and bank allocations are unchanged. The posted Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet continue to use posted journal entries only.
- The Accountant Pack includes `03A_Confirmed_Purchase_Items.csv` alongside the original supplier bill schedule. The current Xero purchase CSV refuses reviewed bills with asset, stock or changed line categories until a suitable accountant-approved account mapping exists.

## Apply in this order

1. Check that the existing `se_invoice_reviews` and `se_review_invoice` operational objects from `docs/STOCK-EQUIPMENT-INVOICE-ITEM-REVIEW-DRAFT.sql` have already been installed in the target database. This package's v61.89 stock frontend already depends on them. Validate v61.90 against a schema-matching staging copy before main.
2. Apply `supabase/migrations/20260924130000_v6190_purchase_review_core.sql`. This creates the general Expenses review independently of the optional module and copies previously confirmed register reviews into the general history. It does not switch the optional module on.
3. Deploy the updated `supabase/functions/scan-expense-document/index.ts` with its existing environment variables. Then deploy the frontend as one release. Deploying a frontend with no migration causes the new review query to fail.
4. In a non-production tenant, test a single photo; a two-photo bill; a multipage PDF; mixed taxable/no-GST items; a no-GST bill; one bill with incomplete pages; a changed category; no Stock entitlement; Stock entitlement active; a correction; a paid/bank-matched bill; Accountant Pack; Summary versus posted P&L. Check desktop and phone layout. Do not apply test fixture values to CareClean.

## Evidence and limits

The migration compiled in an **isolated** Supabase project. Transactional fixture checks, rolled back after execution, covered two attachments, duplicate-page rejection, exact bill totals, rejected partial saves, stale revision rejection, frozen confirmed scans, unchanged source values, module off (zero register reviews), and module on (one register review). Local `node --test tests/*.test.js`: **20/20**; JavaScript syntax checks passed on touched browser scripts. The isolated project has zero actual customer businesses, so this is not a full current production staging clone. The SQL was subsequently applied to main and the Edge Function updated to v12 after the v61.90C investigation; no existing bill figures were changed. Browser testing and a live AI call were not available in this workspace.

**Remaining accounting work:** Confirmed lines do not post journal reclassifications, cost of sales on sale/use, inventory balances, book/tax depreciation, small tool tax deductions or revised filed returns. Financials Summary remains an estimate; its estimated tax is not a filing calculation when capital items or unsold stock are present. A complete accounting release needs posting rules with idempotent reversals, account mappings, stock movement matching and accountant-approved NZ tax treatment. Do not describe this release as IRD-approved accounting or rely on its estimates for filing.
