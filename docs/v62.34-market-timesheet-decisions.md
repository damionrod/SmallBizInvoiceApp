# v62.34 Market timesheet decisions

This Market-only release closes the employee timesheet review loop without creating another payroll or payment path.

## Included

- Atomic manager approval and send-back for one or up to 100 selected employee submissions.
- Required feedback when a timesheet is sent back.
- Employee visibility of that feedback and correction/resubmission of the same record.
- Idempotent decision retries, stale-batch refusal, self-review refusal and server-side Payroll permission checks.
- Private operation evidence plus the existing payroll audit log.
- A narrow trigger preventing direct browser decisions or content edits to a submitted portal timesheet.

## Deliberately unchanged

- Manager-created timesheet entry and expense workflows.
- Existing pay-run calculation, approval, finalisation and ledger posting.
- Employee access remains optional and off by default.
- Approval does not prove payment and does not automatically add the hours to a pay run.
- Main/live, external providers and Stripe are outside this release.

## Acceptance

Offline regression, Market build, managed rollback fixture, security-advisor review, exact-tree publication, CI and protected Market deployment are required before completion is claimed. Authenticated browser/device testing and concurrent multi-session decisions remain separate commercial acceptance gates.
